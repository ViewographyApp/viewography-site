Features

Up Next

The next episode of every show you're following and the movies you've saved, filtered to the services you pay for.

A Watchlist that sorts itself

What you can watch now, what's coming soon, what's in theaters, and what you can rent or buy.

What to Watch

Suggestions based on your services and taste, plus Explore for browsing movies and TV by where they stream, genre and year.

Your services, organized

Paid subscriptions, add-on channels, free services and TV access, so Viewography knows what you can actually watch.

Episode tracking

Mark episodes or whole seasons watched, set the date you watched them, start a show partway through, and undo anything.

Stats and challenges

Your viewing life in numbers, with milestones and challenges along the way.

Bring your history from Trakt

Import your watch history, ratings and watchlist from a public Trakt profile in a few minutes. Nothing is sent back to Trakt.

Private by design

Your library lives on your iPhone with a backup in your own iCloud. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Coming in the next beta

In testing now. Details may change before release.

  • Rating takes a deliberate tap. Stars on a title page no longer change when you scroll past them. Tap Rate, pick your stars and save, with Undo if you change your mind.

Release notes

S2E5 · Everything Everywhere All at Once

2.0 (23) Apple iOS beta
  • Watch Later. Keep a title on your Watchlist but out of Up Next, in its own Watch Later rows. Watching it, or removing Watch Later, brings it back.
  • Where each episode streams. When a show is split across services, Viewography shows where the episode you're up to can be watched, including new episodes on networks through TV Access.
  • Episode details. Tap an episode for its image, summary, cast and crew, and where to watch it. Tap anyone for their filmography. New episodes from the last 14 days are marked NEW.
  • A redesigned Explore. One row of filters for where it streams, genre and year, with results you can watch in the US.
  • Up Next fits on one screen on current iPhones.
  • After an import, Up Next fills in first, and a large library finishes in minutes instead of hours.
  • Long-running shows load every season, and you can mark a season unwatched.
Previous builds

S2E4 · Back to the Future

2.0 (22) Apple iOS beta
  • Trakt import is easier: find it anytime in Settings, paste your profile link or type your username (underscores work), and get clear help if your profile is private. The last step now says exactly what it will add.
  • While a big import fills in, Up Next and Watchlist show "Getting your library ready" with progress.
  • Settings is organized into simple panels.
  • Streaming services have a new Add-ons tab for channels you get through Prime Video, Apple TV or Roku. Network apps like Bravo, FX and TCM now sit under TV Access.
  • "Find more" on Up Next opens just that kind of title, with a button for all of What to Watch.
  • Long titles fit neatly on posters.
  • Watchlist shows "Checking availability…" while it checks, and movies in theaters move out of "Availability to check" more reliably.
  • Movies that aren't out yet no longer offer Watched At or Owned.
  • Viewography is now iPhone only.
